## RULES (from memory + house style)

- **Under 300KB.** Check the actual file size before calling it done — do not trust an HTTP 200.
- WebP format.
- No Apple logos or recognisable branding. Avoids trademark trouble and looks less like an ad.
- **No medical or surgical imagery.** The article discusses organ trafficking as a viral myth; illustrating it would be an AdSense risk. Keep all images about money, phones and people.
- Real-looking people in real settings. No sterile studio stock.
- Compress with Squoosh or TinyPNG before uploading.
